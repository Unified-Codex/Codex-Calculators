    Spliced stat combinations

    A Riven Splicer consumes two matching stats on one riven and replaces them with the spliced stat plus one random stat. Only one spliced stat can be on a riven. Where a stat lists more than one recipe, either pair works.

    Base stat values

    Switch between positive and negative ranges, then pick a weapon category to see its base values and roll ranges for every riven layout.

    The whole formula

    Final value = base value × layout multiplier × roll

    Every riven stat is these three numbers multiplied together. Only the roll is random.

    Step 1: Base value

    Each stat has a base value that depends on the weapon category. Rifle damage is 198%, rifle critical chance is about 180%, and so on. Shotguns, pistols, archguns and melee weapons each have their own base values, and some stats only exist on certain categories. The full list is in the Show Base Stat Values table.

    Step 2: Layout multiplier

    How many positive and negative stats the riven has changes how strong each stat is. The multiplier is the same for every positive stat on the riven, and the negative stat has its own value.

    LayoutPositiveNegativeWhat it means
    2 pos, 0 neg0.99noneEach stat is at about 99% strength
    2 pos, 1 neg1.2375-0.495Each stat is boosted to 123.75%
    3 pos, 0 neg0.75noneThree stats share the power, so each is 75%
    3 pos, 1 neg0.9375-0.75Each stat is at 93.75% strength

    Adding a negative boosts every positive by 25% compared to the same riven without one: 0.99 × 1.25 = 1.2375 and 0.75 × 1.25 = 0.9375. Those starting numbers (0.99, 0.75) are tuning values rather than something derived from a deeper formula.

    Step 3: The random roll

    Each stat rolls between 90% and 110% of its value, independently. That is where the min and max you see in the calculator come from: multiply by 0.9 for the lowest roll and by 1.1 for the highest.

    Putting it together

    Take the stat's base value for your weapon category, multiply by the layout multiplier to get the center value, then apply the 0.9 to 1.1 roll for the low and high ends.

    Worked example

    A rifle riven with 3 positive and 1 negative stat.

    Positive: damage (198% base)

    198 × 0.9375 = 185.6% center
    Low roll: 185.6 × 0.9 = 167.1%
    High roll: 185.6 × 1.1 = 204.2%

    Negative: a stat with a 108% base

    108 × -0.75 = -81% center
    Low roll: -81 × 0.9 = -72.9%
    High roll: -81 × 1.1 = -89.1%

    The negative uses its own value from the layout table, which is why it has a separate multiplier from the positives.
